## SLIDE 2

So I first asked a very simple question: which payment methods are actually paying back the subsidy?

At first glance, the answer seems obvious: stop investing in BNPL and Wallet, and put more money into Credit Installment.”

“But I don't think that's the right conclusion.”
## SLIDE 3

“Because a payment method can lose money for two very different reasons.”

“It can be losing money because we are buying incremental behavior.”

“Or it can be losing money because we are subsidizing users who would have purchased anyway.”


When I broke the loss down by user segment, I found that a large portion of the loss comes from voucher hunters.


There is also a meaningful group of normal customers using BNPL and Wallet frequently.

These users may already be loyal. If they are going to purchase anyway, continuing to subsidize them creates very little incremental value

This suggests an opportunity to distinguish between subsidy that changes behavior and subsidy that simply discounts behavior that would have happened anyway.”
## SLIDE 4
And then Credit Installment gives us a very different story.
9% platform discount

$41.5 AOV

+$3.36 net position/order
“It generates the highest AOV, requires the lowest platform discount, and is already net positive.”
So I don't think Credit Installment needs more subsidy.

Instead of putting more money into something that already works, I would let it grow organically.
## SLIDE 5

Sau slide 5, bạn nói:

“So at this point, I don't think the answer is simply to cut subsidy from BNPL and Wallet.”

“The more important question is: where can we control the subsidy without hurting the underlying demand?”

Rồi đưa vào insight Sale Day:

“When I looked at Sale Day behavior, I found something interesting.”

“Seller discount and platform discount don't behave in the same way.”

“Seller discount does not significantly increase during Sale Day, while platform discount becomes more aggressive.”

Sau đó:

“This tells me that the platform is currently bearing a relatively large share of the promotional cost.”

Tiếp:

“And when I looked at the top 25% of seller-discounted orders, there is also meaningful overlap with discount-sensitive users.”

Rồi đừng nói ngay “therefore cut subsidy”.

Nói:

“So some of the platform subsidy may be incremental — but some of it may simply overlap with discounts that are already strong enough to drive the transaction.”

🔥 Đây mới là insight quan trọng của slide.

Sau đó mới chốt:

“Therefore, instead of increasing subsidy across all Sale Day orders, I would make the subsidy more targeted.”

Và lúc này mới transition sang slide 7:

“That leads me to two different growth engines.”

“For Credit Installment, we have a clean growth engine.”

“For BNPL and Wallet, we have a controlled cross-subsidy engine.”


## SLIDE 6

Engine 1 — CLEAN GROWTH

“For Credit Installment, I would not increase subsidy.”

“Instead, we invest in checkout experience, installment partner terms, and merchant acquisition.”

Fashion và Beauty là hai hướng để investigate:

Fashion → promising high-value orders nhưng sample nhỏ.
Beauty → volume lớn nhưng distribution skewed.

“These are signals, not conclusions. I would validate them with a larger sample before scaling.”



“For BNPL and Wallet, I wouldn't stop investing.”

“I would simply stop subsidizing everyone equally.”


“For loyal 30+ order users, we taper the discount.”

Vì:

“If a customer has already made 30+ purchases, we have evidence that they don't need a large incentive to transact.”

Case của bạn chỉ ra nhóm 30+ orders đã chiếm khoảng 31% Wallet share và được đề xuất giảm incentive.

Grow

“And we redirect that saved budget toward new users and mid-frequency users — especially the 4–10 order segment.”

Rồi chốt:

“So this is not about spending more money. It is about spending the same money where it has a higher chance of changing behavior.”




“There is one more opportunity I don't want to ignore.”

“These customers already demonstrate strong engagement, and they also adopt Wallet and BNPL.”

“So instead of treating Credit Installment only as a payment method, we can treat it as an acquisition channel for other payment methods.”

Tức là:

CI → Wallet / BNPL

Không phải:

CI → thêm subsidy

Mà:

CI → customer acquisition / cross-sell

## SLIDE 7

“First, let Credit Installment grow on its own — it already pays for itself.”

“Second, don't kill BNPL and Wallet. Instead, move subsidy from customers who don't need it to customers whose behavior can still be changed.”

“And third, don't scale the recommendation based on correlation alone. Prove the incremental impact through a controlled pilot.”
